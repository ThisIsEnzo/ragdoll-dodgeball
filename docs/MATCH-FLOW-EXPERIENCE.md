# Match flow and round clarity

## Purpose

Make the core loop legible without introducing a second match-state system or a
second HUD controller. The server remains authoritative; the existing HUD
subscribes to the existing `MatchState` remote.

## Changes

- `RoundService.RoundIndex` increments once at the start of each round and resets
  only when the match is cleared.
- `MatchService` includes `round` and `winsNeeded` in its existing
  `MatchState` payload.
- The existing `HUDController` presents distinct, short team ceremonies for
  round intro, live start, round completion and sudden death.
- The persistent score/state strip identifies the round during active play.
- Personal KO and return cards are driven by the character's server-owned
  `Benched` attribute. A return card is shown only after the server clears it.
- The existing match-end result panel remains the sole match-result panel.

## Important architecture decisions

- No new RemoteEvent and no client-side match lifecycle.
- No second ScreenGui/controller competing with `HUDController`.
- No camera ownership changes.
- Round number is generated by `RoundService`, not guessed from the client.
- Existing `BenchBehaviour` sets `BenchSeated`; this change does not invent a
  `Seated` attribute or alter bench movement.

## Validation

Automated repository CI is expected to run on the PR. No Roblox Studio runtime
or multiplayer test has been performed in this environment.

## Studio checklist

1. Start a match and confirm the HUD says `ROUND 1 — GET READY`.
2. Confirm the transition to `GO! — ROUND 1 IS LIVE` is unmistakable.
3. During play, confirm the score and current round remain visible.
4. At round end, confirm `ROUND COMPLETE` and the updated score appear.
5. Confirm the following round increments to `ROUND 2`, not `ROUND 1`.
6. Enter sudden death and confirm it has its own distinct warning.
7. Get knocked out: confirm the personal KO card appears without replacing the
   team round state.
8. Have a teammate revive you: confirm `BACK IN PLAY` appears only after
   `Benched` becomes false.
9. Finish a match: confirm the existing victory/defeat panel still appears and
   no round banner remains over it.
10. Repeat on desktop and mobile; verify the banners do not cover action controls.
